# LawMerge

A merge game for mental capacity and mental health law, built for LPS Law Ltd.

Answer a question to earn credits, buy an item, drag two of the same together to level them up, find all seven.

| Scene | Chain | Law |
|---|---|---|
| The care home | The paper trail | Mental Capacity Act and deprivation of liberty |
| The ward | From concern to discharge | Mental Health Act 1983 |
| The court | Up to the Court of Appeal | Court of Protection |

38 questions, each carrying its citation.

Post-AGNI: *Cheshire West* and the "acid test" were set aside by *A Reference by the Attorney General for Northern Ireland* [2026] UKSC 16 with effect from 2 June 2026, so the deprivation of liberty questions apply the multi-factorial *Guzzardi* approach.

- `index.html` — the app. Open it directly or serve the folder; the artwork is embedded as base64 in the `art-*.js` files, so it runs without a network connection once loaded (webfonts fall back to system fonts offline).
